02 / PHONE SCG

A recording from your chest.

Record a still phone on your sternum using an accelerometer logger. Import 20 to 300 seconds at 50 to 500 Hz. CSV columns: t,ax,ay,az; seconds and m/s².

Processed locally by Python. Imported recordings do not control a live game.

What this does not measure

These are optical and mechanical signals, not electrical ECG. BPM, spectral concentration and channel agreement are research measurements, not a diagnosis, blood pressure or a stress score.
